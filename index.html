Why most people stall

Effort is not the problem. Programming is.

Everyone wants to feel better and look better, and plenty of people are willing to work for it. What most are missing is a plan that progresses. Months of YouTube workouts and whatever machine is open is not a program, and your body knows the difference.

The approach

No cookie cutter anything

I bring the same philosophy to every client. How it gets implemented is different for all of them. Your program is built around your body, your schedule, and the goal you actually care about.

  • Proper periodization. Training blocks that build on each other instead of restarting every week.
  • Progressive overload. The one variable that decides whether you grow, tracked every session.
  • Exercise selection for your body. Chosen for your leverages, history, and equipment, not for a highlight reel.
  • Peer reviewed, not trending. Every rep scheme and rest period comes out of the research, not a reel.
A 2024 review of 218 trials and 14,170 people found strength training produced a moderate reduction in depressive symptoms, and was among the best tolerated forms of exercise studied.4

Strength training came out at a Hedges' g of −0.49 against active controls, putting it alongside walking, jogging, and yoga near the top of the list. It is also the part of training nobody puts on a before and after photo, and the part clients tell me about first.
